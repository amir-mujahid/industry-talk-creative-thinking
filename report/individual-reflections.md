## Member 2 - jiaxuan

### 1. Personal Reflection on the Industry Talk
The industry talk helped me understand that thinking skills are important not only in studying but also in our future careers. I learned that creative thinking allows us to come up with different ideas, while critical thinking helps us analyse and choose the best solution. The speaker also introduced mathematical thinking, scientific thinking and metacognition.

One topic that I found interesting was metacognition, which means thinking about our own thinking. It teaches us to plan, monitor and evaluate our progress. I realised that solving a problem is not only about getting the correct answer, but also understanding the steps we take to reach it.

Overall, this talk reminded me that I should not depend only on memorising information. I need to learn how to think, ask questions and improve myself.

### 2. Knowledge and Skills Gained
From this talk, I gained a better understanding of several important thinking skills.

Creative Thinking: Developing new ideas and finding alternative solutions.

Critical Thinking: Analysing information and evaluating different choices before making decisions.

Mathematical Thinking: Using logical reasoning, patterns and calculations to solve problems.

Scientific Thinking: Using observation, experiments and evidence to understand problems.

Metacognition: Planning, monitoring and evaluating my own learning process.

I also learned about Polya's Problem-Solving Model, which includes understanding the problem, solving the problem and looking back at the solution. These skills can help me become a better learner and problem solver.

### 3. Application to Computer Science and Data Engineering
As a Data Engineering student, I believe these thinking skills are useful in my future studies and career. For example, creative thinking can help me find different ways to design data pipelines and improve data processing. Critical thinking allows me to identify errors and check whether the data is reliable. Metacognition also allows me to review my work, identify mistakes and improve my coding skills.

### 4. Three-Year Personal Development Plan

#### Year 1
I will focus on building a strong foundation in programming, mathematics and computer science. I will practise coding regularly and improve my problem-solving skills through assignments and exercises.

#### Year 2
I plan to improve my technical skills in Python, SQL, databases and cloud computing. I will also participate in group projects to gain more experience in teamwork and applying my knowledge to real problems.

#### Year 3
I hope to gain practical experience through internships and real-world projects. I will also explore professional certifications related to data engineering to prepare myself for the working environment.

### 5. Expected Career Goals
My goal is to become a Data Engineer in the future. I hope to develop strong technical skills in programming, database management and data processing. In the future, I hope to work in the technology industry, gain more experience and continue learning about new technologies.
